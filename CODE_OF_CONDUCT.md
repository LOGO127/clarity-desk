# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Give and accept technical feedback in good faith.
- Protect other people's recordings, transcripts, identities, and credentials.
- Do not pressure anyone to share private interview material.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, publication of private information, and misuse of the project for covert surveillance are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behavior violates this code. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's maintainer contact or GitHub reporting tools.

This code is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
